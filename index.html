Profile

Strategy that survives volatile markets.

Resilient organizations outperform.

A transformational leader with a track record of steering multi-billion-dollar commercial operations in Bangladesh's steel sector. He translates macro-economic infrastructure trends into practical business strategy that delivers sustainable P&L growth and market share gains.
